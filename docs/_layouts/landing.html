What it does

A coach that
actually reads your data.

Not another generic plan generator. VO2.ai talks to your model of choice with the context of every run you've actually done.

💬
Conversational coaching

Ask anything — pacing strategy, what yesterday's tempo run actually means, how to adjust this week. The AI sees your Strava history and replies with context, not platitudes.

🔑
Bring your own key

Paste your OpenRouter API key once. Pick the model you trust — GPT, Gemini, Claude, or open weights. Every chat goes straight from your phone to the model. We never see it.

Compatible with STRAVA
Strava-native

Authorize once via OAuth. Recent activities are pulled in so the coach can see what you actually ran this week — pace, distance, heart rate, all of it.

📅
Plans that bend

Ask for a 16-week marathon block, a 5K speed plan, or a "get back into running after illness" reset. Adjust on the fly when life gets in the way — the plan moves with you.

Privacy by design
0 servers.

The full count of VO2.ai infrastructure.

No accounts.
No analytics.
No backend.

There is no VO2.ai server. The app talks directly to Strava, directly to OpenRouter, and stores everything else locally on your device. Your API key lives in the iOS Keychain. Your chat history, plans, and profile live in a local database that you can wipe with one tap in Settings → Delete Account & Data. Because the entire source code is public, you can verify every network call and every piece of stored data yourself.

How it works

Three steps.
Your data stays yours.

01
Connect Strava

Authorize once via OAuth. Recent runs are pulled into the app so the coach can see what you actually ran.

02
Bring your AI key

Paste your OpenRouter key. Pick GPT, Gemini, Claude — whatever you trust. Stored only in the iOS Keychain.

03
Train with a coach

Ask for a plan. Discuss yesterday's tempo. Adjust when life happens. Like texting a running friend who reads your data.
